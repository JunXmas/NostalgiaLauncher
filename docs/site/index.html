    BEFORE YOU BEGIN

    You might be wondering.

    Is Nostalgia free?

    Yes. Instance management, the content library, core launcher features, multiplayer, and hosting servers on your own machine are free. Nostalgia+ is an optional supporter plan; you can review its benefits and price in the launcher before buying.

    Does Google replace my Microsoft account?

    No. Google is used for your Nostalgia account, friends, and premium benefits. To play with a Microsoft Minecraft account, you still need to sign in to Microsoft and own the appropriate edition of the game.

    Can friends sync if only the host has Plus?

    Yes. A host with Plus or above can enable modpack sharing. Friends accept the invitation and choose what to install. Host eligibility is checked by the backend. Only accept mods from people you trust.

    Is Server Pro a rented cloud server?

    No. The launcher helps you create, configure, and run a server on your own machine. Mod and plugin compatibility depends on the platform and version; connections from outside your network also depend on your network setup.

    How do I upgrade from an earlier plan?

    Choose a higher plan in the launcher. The backend calculates credit for the unused portion of your paid purchase and shows the price before you create an order. Renewing the same plan uses the regular price; gifted benefits do not generate credit.

    Are benefits activated as soon as I pay?

    Vietcombank payments are currently approved manually after the received funds are verified. The “I have transferred” button only requests a payment review; it does not activate a plan automatically.

    What account information does the app use?

    Google sign-in uses your email, name, and avatar to create a Nostalgia account. The service stores data needed for friends, profiles, sign-in sessions, and supporter plans. You can play and manage basic instances without buying premium.
